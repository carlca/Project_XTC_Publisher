Most difficulties people experience with the X-Touch come from thinking of it as a mixer with fixed controls.

## Bitwig Is Always in Charge

The X-Touch follows Bitwig's current state.

::: reality-check

If the X-Touch behaves unexpectedly, check the selected track, current mode and scribble strips first.

:::
